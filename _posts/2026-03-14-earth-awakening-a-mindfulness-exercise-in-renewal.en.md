**Earth Awakening** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is renewal. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 11 — Earth Awakening

**Theme:** Renewal

**Prompt:**  
Step outside and pause for a moment. Listen to the quiet signals of returning life: birdsong, distant calls, gentle movement in branches. Notice what is just beginning to emerge.

**Reflections:**  
- What sounds signal renewal to me?
- What is beginning to take root in my life, slowly but surely?
- Can I allow this process to unfold at its own pace?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
