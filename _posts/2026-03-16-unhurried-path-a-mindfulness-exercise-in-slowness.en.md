**Unhurried Path** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is slowness. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 3 — Unhurried Path

**Theme:** Slowness

**Prompt:**  
Walk at half your usual pace. Let your breath set the rhythm. With each step, fully feel the ground beneath you.

**Reflections:**  
- What do I begin to notice only when I am not rushing anywhere?
- Where in my life am I moving forward without need?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
