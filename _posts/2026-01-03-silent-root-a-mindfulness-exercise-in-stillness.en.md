**Silent Root** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is stillness. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 1 — Silent Root

**Theme:** Stillness

**Prompt:**  
Enter a green space slowly. Pause often. Sit or stand still for five minutes.

**Reflections:**  
- What sounds remain when everything grows quiet?
- Where in my body do I feel stillness?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
