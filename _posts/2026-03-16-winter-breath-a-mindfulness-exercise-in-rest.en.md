**Winter Breath** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is rest. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 5 — Winter Breath

**Theme:** Rest

**Prompt:**  
Sit by a window or outside. Observe the softness of each breath.

**Reflections:**  
- What might it mean to rest without guilt?
- Which thoughts keep circling, and which can I let go of now?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
