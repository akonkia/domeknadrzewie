**Hidden Strength** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is endurance. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 2 — Hidden Strength

**Theme:** Endurance

**Prompt:**  
Look for signs of endurance — evergreen needles, moss, lichen, tightly closed buds. Touch them gently.

**Reflections:**  
- Who or what has helped me through difficult seasons?
- In what situations do I feel a sense of inner calm?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
