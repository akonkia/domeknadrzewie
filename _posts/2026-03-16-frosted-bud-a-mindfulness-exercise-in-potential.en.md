**Frosted Bud** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is potential. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 7 — Frosted Bud

**Theme:** Potential

**Prompt:**  
Find a shrub or tree and look closely at its buds — small promises waiting for the right moment.

**Reflections:**  
- What is quietly maturing within me?
- What is the smallest sign that something is beginning?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
