**Hidden Spark** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is subtle energy. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 6 — Hidden Spark

**Theme:** Subtle Energy

**Prompt:**  
Look for small places where warmth gathers. It might be a ray of sunlight on a wall, or a scarf warmed by your breath.

**Reflections:**  
- What warmth do I carry within me?
- Where in my life is something beginning?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
