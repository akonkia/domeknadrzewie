**Morning Whispers** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is small wonders. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 12 — Morning Whispers

**Theme:** Small Wonders

**Prompt:**  
Spend a few minutes each morning sitting by a window or beneath a tree. Close your eyes, breathe gently, and let your attention follow the sounds of early spring — birdsong, rustling branches, the subtle pulse of awakening life. Notice which of them bring you calm, curiosity, or lightness.

**Reflections:**  
- Which small joys speak to me most right now?
- How do these subtle moments shape my mood or the way I enter the day?
- Where else during the day can I pause to hear this quiet music around me?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
