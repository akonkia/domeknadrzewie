**Thaw** is this week's urban bathing card. Its guiding theme is softening. The practice is meant to stay simple: a few quiet minutes, one clear observation, and a little room to remain with what is already present around you.

## Week 10 — Thaw

**Theme:** Softening

**Prompt:**  
Step outside and look for signs of thaw: small puddles, darkening bark, the quiet sheen of wet earth.

**Reflections:**  
- Where in my life can I safely soften a little?
- What changes when I allow this moment to be exactly as it is, without holding on or resisting?

## How to approach this practice

You do not need to add much more to this card than what it already suggests. A few minutes, a little presence, and a willingness to return to it later in the week are enough if you want to see whether it lands differently the second time.
