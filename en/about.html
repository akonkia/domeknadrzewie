A project close to nature

Resources, reflections and education inspired by nature

Project Tree House was created out of the need to build a place and materials that bring people closer to nature. We believe that nature has an extraordinary power to regulate, calm and support people, regardless of age or experience.

About

We create materials that help people discover the world of plants: simple, accessible, inspiring and rooted in respect for one another and for nature.

We want everyone to be able to feel this connection, even for a moment, even in the rush of everyday life, even in the city.

Our tree house is a symbol of a safe place, light between leaves and a moment of pause.

Today we are building the foundations: knowledge, resources and ideas that inspire greater mindfulness.
