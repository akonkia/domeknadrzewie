Contact

If you would like to write about materials, collaboration, an idea for a new resource, or simply to say hello, I would be very glad to hear from you.

The Tree House Project grows slowly, like a walk rather than a sprint. I am always glad to hear from people looking for calm materials inspired by nature, mindfulness, seasonality, and everyday contact with the world around us.

Write if you…

  • are looking for materials related to nature and mindfulness,
  • work with children and want to share what is missing for you,
  • have an idea for collaboration,
  • would like to use my materials in your own work,
  • want to share a reflection, suggestion, or kind word.

For teachers and educators

If you work in a preschool, school, library, or non-formal education setting and are looking for nature-inspired materials, feel free to get in touch. I would love to know which themes, formats, or resources would be genuinely useful in your work with children.
